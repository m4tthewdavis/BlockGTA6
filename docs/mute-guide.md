# Safehouse: block GTA 6 on your phone in 5 minutes

*Your safehouse from spoilers.*

GTA 6 comes out on **19 November 2026**. These steps use each app's **own built-in filters**.
You don't need to install anything, and they work on **iPhone and Android** right now.

Older GTA games (GTA 5, GTA Online, the original Vice City) won't be affected unless you add the
optional words in step 1.

> Menu names change between app versions and regions. If a path doesn't match exactly, search the
> app's Settings for "mute", "hidden words" or "keywords".

## 2. TikTok
**Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Content preferences → Filter video keywords → Add**

- Add each word from step 1, and choose **For You** *and* **Following**.
- If you see **Smart Keyword Filters**, turn it on. It also catches similar wording.
- TikTok says these filters *reduce* how often a topic appears rather than removing it completely.
  Press and hold any GTA 6 video that still gets through → **Not interested**.

## 3. X (Twitter)
**Settings and privacy → Privacy and safety → Mute and block → Muted words → ＋**

- Add each word. Set **Home timeline** ✅ + **Notifications** ✅, **From anyone**, duration **Forever**.
- Muted words may **not** apply to **Search** or **Explore/Trends**, so avoid those tabs for a while.

## 4. Threads
**Profile → ☰ → Settings → Privacy (or Content preferences) → Hidden Words**

- Add the words. Threads now hides them in the **feed, search, profiles and replies**.
- Threads can give filters a 30-day timer. Make sure yours are set *not* to expire (or renew them).

## 5. Instagram
Instagram has two separate filters. Use both:

1. **Suggested posts and Reels:** on any GTA 6 post tap **⋯ → Not interested → Don't suggest posts
   with certain words**, then add the words from step 1. (You can also find it under Settings →
   **Content preferences**.) This only affects *recommended* content, not accounts you follow.
2. **Comments and messages:** **Settings → Hidden Words → Manage custom words and phrases**, and
   add the same words.

If you follow gaming accounts that will post about GTA 6, mute them for a while
(profile → Following → Mute).

## 6. YouTube (no keyword mute, so use these instead)
1. On every GTA 6 video you see: **⋮ → Not interested** *and* **⋮ → Don't recommend channel**
   for channels that mainly cover GTA 6.
2. **Turn off notifications** (🔔 → None) for gaming-news channels and **Rockstar Games**, or
   unsubscribe until you've played.
3. Delete any GTA 6 searches from **search history** (Settings → Manage all history).
4. **Strongest option:** turn **watch history off** (You → Settings → Manage all history →
   Turn off). If you don't have much existing history, YouTube shows a **blank Home feed** and
   removes "Up next" recommendations. Your Subscriptions tab still works. If your Home feed still
   shows videos after turning it off, also delete your past watch history.
5. For full keyword blocking, watch YouTube in a browser with the Safehouse extension (coming soon).

## 7. Reddit
Reddit has **no keyword mute**. Instead:
- Leave and **mute** the GTA 6 communities: open **r/GTA6** and **r/GTAVI** → **⋯ → Mute community**.
- Consider muting general gaming-news and leak communities until you've played.
- Full keyword filtering on Reddit is coming with the Safehouse extension.

## 8. Google app / Discover feed
On any GTA 6 card: **⋮ → Not interested in "Grand Theft Auto VI"** (or "Don't show content from this site").

## 9. Notifications (an easy one to miss)
A notification title ("New video: GTA 6 ending explained…") can spoil things without you opening anything.
- **iPhone:** Settings → Notifications → **Show Previews → Never**. Or turn off notifications for
  YouTube, X, Reddit and news apps for a while.
- **Android:** Settings → Notifications → Lock screen → **Don't show sensitive content**
  (wording varies by phone), and silence YouTube/X/Reddit/news apps for a while.

---

## 10. ⚠️ Watch out for scams
There is **no GTA 6 phone app**. Apps or downloads promising "GTA 6 Beta", "GTA 6 Mobile" or early
access are scams that steal accounts or install malware. Don't install them.
